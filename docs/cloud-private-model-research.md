# Private hosted model research

Private stock and crypto research now runs in the private `FiNdAlMkSkInDaL/Macro-Bias-Model-Monitor` repository on Wednesdays at 11:17 UTC. It works with the laptop and app off. The private [research ledger](https://github.com/FiNdAlMkSkInDaL/Macro-Bias-Model-Monitor/issues/2) is canonical; the local research state is retained as historical evidence and must not be used to duplicate experiments. The former local weekly task is paused after hosted verification.

The compute job has read-only GitHub permissions and no production database, collector, email or deployment credentials. It uses a pinned research source revision and locked dependencies with lifecycle scripts disabled. The existing subscriber/scoring source hashes and forward-model fingerprint are checked before and after. No subscriber generation, template, sending schedule, published score or current forward-test recipe is changed. Planning and evidence publication run in separate private-repository jobs; no job can deploy to this application.

Inputs are a private, hash-verified snapshot of previously inspected historical data through 26 October 2026. Later forward outcomes remain reserved for evaluation, not candidate selection. The setup replay must exactly reproduce the previously saved stock and crypto calibration results before new experiments are considered operational. A successful reproduction verifies the research machinery, not a new predictive edge.

At the owner's request on 27 October 2026, the private policy's second revision expands the two original hypotheses to **14 weekly experiments**, one per Wednesday through 27 January 2027. The original two recipes and existing evidence are preserved. Twelve further recipes are declared before results are calculated: fixed base-rate shrinkage, own-price analogues, macro-only and slow/fast features, risk-adjusted inputs, bounded training labels, raw-return fitting, stronger regularization, trend/macro interactions, broader neighbours and a fixed linear/local ensemble. Exact recipes and ordering live in the private versioned policy and canonical ledger; the queue is not chosen from backtest winners.

Each candidate is evaluated for both markets with matching publication-aligned target sessions, simple and existing model benchmarks, return/probability losses, annual and recent-period slices, uncertainty and zero/base/double trading costs. Full recipes, failures, aggregate reports and per-date evidence are retained privately with data/code fingerprints. A failed or missed slot blocks new work until inspected; no silent batch catch-up or replacement of losing recipes. All 14 slots run experiments rather than reverting to integrity-only checks after week two.

The larger search does not turn reused history into an untouched holdout. Historical paired intervals are descriptive and unadjusted across the 14 recipes and two markets. Reports record this selection risk; they cannot establish a new live edge. See [Bailey et al., The Probability of Backtest Overfitting](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/4w1110bb). Any nomination needs a separately declared future test with appropriate comparison control. The existing forward-test outcomes stay reserved for their original fixed models and are never used to select, tune or validate these new candidates.

The independent hourly owner-email monitor reads research results, failed/stalled runs and missing weekly checkpoints through the separate private ledger. Routine successful audits stay quiet. A promising historical result may only nominate a distinct future private forward experiment. The owner subsequently authorized automatic private enrollment under `private-candidate-forward-tests.md`; automatic production promotion remains prohibited and requires a new owner decision. Captures in the existing forward experiment are not extended. The research workflow stops when its fixed window ends on 3 February 2027.

See the private repository's [research workflow](https://github.com/FiNdAlMkSkInDaL/Macro-Bias-Model-Monitor/actions/workflows/private-research.yml), README and versioned policy for the exact queue, permissions, private input release and evidence records. GitHub scheduling can delay jobs; no paid model API or plan upgrade is introduced by this migration.
