# Automatic private candidate forward tests

On 27 October 2026 the owner authorized automatic enrollment of promising research candidates into a separate private forward experiment. This authorizes evaluation only. Live scores, subscriber emails, sending schedules and the original `mb_model_forward_20261027_v1` experiment remain unchanged. There is no automatic production promotion.

The private `Macro-Bias-Model-Monitor` repository runs `candidate-forward.yml` at 09:17 UTC, with an 11:17 recovery slot, every day with the laptop off. It reads the canonical historical research ledger and the checksum-verified immutable report. Admission is per market: only the existing `NOMINATE_SEPARATE_PRIVATE_FORWARD_TEST_ONLY` decision, all seven historical checks passing, and improved return/probability losses versus all three simple baselines can enroll. A stock-only result does not enroll crypto. The 14 fixed recipes, historical data cutoff and pre-existing historical results are not changed or selected from the current forward test.

Each candidate/market can enroll once. Its private declaration fixes the recipe, research commit and report hash, execution/policy fingerprint, benchmark family, first capture time and end dates before computing forecasts. The first capture is no earlier than 09:00 UTC the following day. It gets 120 calendar days of capture and seven days for settlement. This gives late research discoveries time to be evaluated, independently of the original experiment's February stop. New admissions stop on 3 February 2027; the private candidate process has a hard stop on 10 June 2027, and can stop earlier after all admitted cohorts finish.

The private catalog is [issue 5](https://github.com/FiNdAlMkSkInDaL/Macro-Bias-Model-Monitor/issues/5). Quote vintages, locked predictions, monthly fits, settled quotes, reports and checkpoint-chain hashes are preserved in private release assets. Later jobs cannot overwrite a saved target prediction or outcome. GitHub's release publication time must precede the stock exchange open or crypto UTC target open; a delayed lock is retained as failed evidence and excluded, never backdated. Fits only use mature targets strictly before the source date; monthly coefficients/neighbour populations are preserved rather than rebuilt from revised quotes.

`GET /api/monitor/research-prices` supplies only completed market quote history, version, read completeness, timestamp and source revision. It requires the existing dedicated `MODEL_MONITOR_READ_TOKEN` header, rejects anonymous/collector credentials before reads, and returns no-store/noindex responses. It reuses the existing validated quote reader without invoking collection, scoring, subscriber generation or writes. The read token now also permits this completed-public-market-data projection; it still has no write, subscriber or deployment capability. Compute gets downloaded JSON and read-only repository access, without database, collector, email or deployment credentials.

Accuracy is evaluated on the candidate's own future matched target sessions against zero/50:50, trailing mature averages and the recipe's own training-base forecast. Existing challenger, momentum and reconstructed production arms are captured as additional diagnostics. The two planned looks are the first 60 locked observations, once all are settled, and the fixed final window. MSE and Brier loss must both improve against all three simple baselines, with paired 20-observation block intervals and improvement in each of the latest two 20-observation blocks. Conservative tails account for 14 recipes × 2 markets × 3 baselines × 2 losses × 2 looks (336 comparisons); 200,000 deterministic bootstrap replications are used. These are approximate uncertainty estimates, not guaranteed simultaneous coverage or independent validation. Net returns at zero/base/double costs are diagnostics, not the accuracy admission rule or a profitability claim.

No repeated weekly winner selection, recipe tuning, outcome backfilling or use of the original forward experiment to choose candidates is allowed. Missing outcomes remain pending/failed, never zero. Missing captures are reported as gaps and prevent an improvement claim. At a deadline, insufficient or incomplete data produces an explicit incomplete final result.

The independent hourly owner monitor reports enrollment, the two reviews, new failures and recoveries to the existing chosen owner email through its durable outbox. It checks stale/missing daily checkpoints and catalog access even if the candidate collector fails. Original forecast capture is not extended; only owner monitoring remains active for the separate candidate cohorts. Routine checks are quiet. The local application need not be open; GitHub/provider outages can still delay jobs or alerts.

Validation covers all 14 live adapters against their frozen historical implementations, future-label poisoning, preserved monthly fits, per-market admission and deduplication, before-open receipts, repeat capture, causal settlement, gap/missing-data handling, the two-look/multiplicity policy, private workflow permissions, endpoint authentication and unchanged subscriber/model source fingerprints. Synthetic fixtures must never be enrolled or stored as actual forward predictions.
